# Hackathon compliance notes

Checked against the official rules on 17 October 2026 (Australia/Melbourne).

- No match data or footage is committed. Use only footage/audio with all necessary rights and permissions.
- The UI labels observations and insights as AI-generated and reports provider failures explicitly.
- Avoid third-party league or club trademarks unless licensed.
- The project must be newly built, functional as depicted, and released in a public GitHub repository for submission.
- Judges need free, unrestricted access to a working site/demo/test build through the judging period.
- The public demo video must be shorter than two minutes, show the functioning project, and be hosted at an accepted public URL.
- Submission materials must be in English or include English translations.
- Confirm entrant and team eligibility separately; this repository cannot establish it.

This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice. Re-check the official rules before submission.
